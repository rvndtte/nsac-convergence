# Abandoned but not Forgotten

**Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars**

Proyek ini mengajak anak-anak sekolah menjadi **Detektif Antariksa**: menyelidiki benda-benda buatan manusia yang tertinggal di Bulan dan Mars, lalu menemukan cerita tentang misi, teknologi, dan orang-orang di baliknya.

Alih-alih menganggapnya sebagai sampah, kami melihat setiap artefak sebagai kapsul waktu—jejak keberanian manusia dalam menjelajahi dunia lain.

## Tentang proyek

Proyek ini dikembangkan untuk tantangan NASA Space Apps 2026 **“Abandoned but not Forgotten”**. Tujuan kami adalah menyampaikan sejarah dan nilai ilmiah peralatan yang ditinggalkan di luar Bumi melalui cerita yang mudah dipahami, menarik, dan didukung sumber data NASA.

Pengalaman proyek direncanakan dalam salah satu atau gabungan format berikut:

### Galactic Artifact Explorer

Aplikasi web interaktif bergaya dashboard detektif. Pengguna dapat menjelajahi artefak, membaca berkas misi, melihat gambar, dan mengungkap fakta tentang kondisi atau nasib peralatan tersebut.

### Buku Catatan Misi Rahasia

Logbook digital bergaya jurnal penjelajah yang menceritakan artefak melalui catatan harian, ilustrasi, foto, dan fakta sains. Logbook dapat disajikan sebagai PDF interaktif atau situs yang bisa digulir.

## Artefak yang dapat diselidiki

Daftar akhir artefak akan ditentukan setelah riset dan kurasi. Kandidat awal:

- **Opportunity (“Oppy”)** — rover Mars yang berhenti berkomunikasi setelah badai debu global pada 2018.
- **Lunar Roving Vehicle** — kendaraan yang digunakan astronaut dalam misi Apollo di Bulan.
- Artefak lain yang relevan dari misi NASA ke Bulan atau Mars.

Setiap berkas artefak akan menjelaskan apa bendanya, misi yang membawanya, alasan benda itu tertinggal, dan warisan atau fakta menariknya. Informasi akan diperiksa berdasarkan sumber tepercaya sebelum dimasukkan.

## Tujuan

- Mengenalkan sejarah eksplorasi Bulan dan Mars kepada anak-anak dengan bahasa sederhana.
- Menyampaikan kisah di balik artefak, bukan hanya spesifikasi teknisnya.
- Menghubungkan setiap cerita dengan fakta dan sumber data NASA.
- Menumbuhkan rasa ingin tahu tentang sains, rekayasa, dan eksplorasi antariksa.

## Teknologi dan alat

Pilihan alat bergantung pada format final:

- **Aplikasi web:** HTML, CSS, dan JavaScript; backend sederhana dengan PHP atau Python/Flask bila diperlukan.
- **Logbook digital:** Canva, Google Slides, atau Figma; ekspor sebagai PDF interaktif atau situs gulir.
- **Demo dan publikasi:** GitHub Pages, hosting sederhana, atau rekaman layar untuk presentasi.

# English

## Abandoned but not Forgotten

**Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars**

This project invites schoolchildren to become **Space Detectives**: investigating human-made objects left behind on the Moon and Mars, and uncovering the missions, technology, and people behind them.

Rather than viewing them as trash, we see each artifact as a time capsule—a trace of humanity's courage to explore other worlds.

## About the project

This project is being developed for the NASA Space Apps 2026 challenge **“Abandoned but not Forgotten.”** Our goal is to share the history and scientific value of equipment left beyond Earth through stories that are accessible, engaging, and supported by NASA data.

The project is planned in one or a combination of the following formats:

### Galactic Artifact Explorer

An interactive web app with a detective-style dashboard. Users can explore artifacts, read mission files, view images, and uncover facts about the equipment's condition or fate.

### Secret Mission Logbook

A digital logbook styled as an explorer's journal, telling artifact stories through diary entries, illustrations, photographs, and science facts. It can be presented as an interactive PDF or a scrolling website.

## Artifacts to investigate

The final list will be chosen after research and curation. Initial candidates include:

- **Opportunity (“Oppy”)** — the Mars rover that stopped communicating after the global dust storm in 2018.
- **Lunar Roving Vehicle** — the vehicle used by astronauts during the Apollo missions on the Moon.
- Other relevant artifacts from NASA missions to the Moon or Mars.

Each artifact file will explain what the object is, which mission brought it there, why it was left behind, and its legacy or other interesting facts. Information will be checked against reliable sources before publication.

## Goals

- Introduce children to the history of lunar and Martian exploration in accessible language.
- Tell the stories behind the artifacts, not just their technical specifications.
- Connect each story to facts and NASA data sources.
- Inspire curiosity about science, engineering, and space exploration.

## Technologies and tools

The tools will depend on the final format:

- **Web app:** HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; a simple PHP or Python/Flask backend if needed.
- **Digital logbook:** Canva, Google Slides, or Figma; export as an interactive PDF or scrolling website.
- **Demo and publication:** GitHub Pages, simple hosting, or a screen recording for the presentation.

## Project plan

### Before the hackathon

1. Select 3–5 compelling artifacts and verify their information sources.
2. Gather facts, mission status, images, and NASA source links.
3. Write child-friendly stories and create a wireframe or logbook layout.

### During the hackathon

1. Build the web app or finalize the logbook design.
2. Review facts, image credits, links, and the user experience.
3. Record a demo/presentation video no longer than 2 minutes.
4. Prepare the repository and submit the project with the data references used.
